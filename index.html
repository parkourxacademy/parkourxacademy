ABOUT PARKOUR X

Movement is more than a sport.

Amr Samy started Parkour in Egypt in 2005 and officially joined the first Parkour team in Egypt on 31/03/2008.

Parkour X was created to share real movement knowledge, build stronger athletes and make Parkour accessible to everyone.

From beginners taking their first step to advanced athletes mastering powerful movements — everyone has a place here.
