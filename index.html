THE STORY

MORE THAN A SPORT.

IT'S A LIFESTYLE.

Amr Samy started his Parkour journey in 2005.

He became a player and one of the founders of the first official Parkour team in Egypt on 31 March 2008. Today, Parkour X brings together training, movement, performances and the Parkour community.

Our goal is simple: make Parkour accessible, exciting and safe for everyone who wants to move.
