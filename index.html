THE STORY

ABOUT PARKOUR X

Amr Samy started his Parkour journey in 2005.

He became part of the first official Parkour team in Egypt on 31/03/2008. Today, Parkour X brings together training, movement, performance and the Parkour community.

Our goal is simple: make Parkour accessible, exciting and safe for everyone who wants to move.
